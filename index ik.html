LOCAL CREATORS + LOCAL MAKERS + GLOBAL DISCOVERY

Idea Kitchen Franchise Studios

Each franchise operates a branded local marketplace and creator studio. It combines Etsy-style seller shops, custom orders, reviews, and local fulfillment with Pinterest-style boards, discovery feeds, saves, follows, and visual inspiration.

3Ways to operate

Home studio, retail creator lab, or regional production hub.

Home Studio

Low-overhead territory owner who recruits local makers, helps creators prepare projects, and earns marketplace and service commissions.

Creator Storefront

A welcoming retail location with displays, consultations, workshops, sample products, pickup, and shipping support.

Regional Production Hub

A larger location with fabrication equipment, quality control, fulfillment, maker training, and support for nearby franchisees.

Franchise platform features

Etsy-like commerce

Independent maker shops, product listings, variants, custom-order requests, reviews, coupons, commissions, local pickup, and shipping.

Pinterest-like discovery

Visual idea feed, boards, saves, follows, related products, public upgrade requests, tutorials, and creator inspiration.

Territory management

Local landing pages, lead routing, protected territories, regional pricing, maker vetting, training, and performance dashboards.

Multiple revenue streams

Franchise fees, royalties, marketplace transaction fees, maker subscriptions, classes, prototyping, fulfillment, promoted listings, and licensing support.

A real franchise offering requires a franchise attorney, Franchise Disclosure Document where applicable, audited financial information, trademarks, operating manuals, training, insurance, and compliance with federal and state rules before selling territories.
